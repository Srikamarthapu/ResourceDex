# Sample photograph credits

These photographs illustrate **clearly labeled sample resources**. They are not photos of inventory available from ResourceDex users, and they must not be attached to a user's live listing. Availability, local area, condition, dimensions and quantity are not established by these source photographs. Sample records should remain marked as examples in cards and details.

All six source pages were checked on September 12, 2026 and identified their photographs as free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), rather than Unsplash+. That license permits downloading, modification and use, including commercial use; it excludes selling unmodified photographs or compiling them into a competing image service. Photographer attribution is retained here. These image files retain their original photo license independently of the application's code license.

Only image-service resizing/WebP compression and the documented metal-photo crop were applied. No objects, damage, surface condition or material details were generated or retouched. Final files were decoded and visually inspected.
